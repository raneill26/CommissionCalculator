03

Rate tables

Reusable tier tables, referenced by rules and components Deal-value tables tier on the size of one deal, in isolation Cumulative tables tier on a running balance across the period (Ex: three $20k deals against $15k bands walk from the first band up to the fourth) Attainment tables tier on percent-of-quota and are used by quota components

04

Rules

Each deal is tested against these rules top to bottom and the first match wins. A rule with no conditions matches everything, keep one at the bottom as a catch-all. This is the section the LLM agent will write for you from a plan document

05

Quota components

Optional, for plans where part of the variable is earned against a quota rather than per deal. Weights are shares of the variable-at-target amount and should total 100%

06

Modifiers

Multipliers applied after everything else (quality scores, adherence ratings, manager discretion) Each can target deal commission, quota commission, or both

07

Cumulative pools
